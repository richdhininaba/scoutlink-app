About Stratex Analytics

Stratex Analytics is building ScoutLink, a football intelligence platform designed to help grassroots players become easier to evidence, compare and discover through coach-led profiles and verified scout workflows.

What ScoutLink is

ScoutLink is a coach-led, verified-scout, safeguarding-conscious grassroots football intelligence platform for U7-U16 player profiles, structured evidence and compatibility-based decision support.

Who it helps

Coaches can organise player evidence; scouts can search and compare context; parents and players can understand how visibility works.

How we describe scores

ScoutLink compatibility scoring is decision support. It does not guarantee trials, signing opportunities or a player's future pathway.

Contact and trust routes

For support, privacy requests or safeguarding concerns, use the public routes so the right Stratex reviewers can triage the issue.
