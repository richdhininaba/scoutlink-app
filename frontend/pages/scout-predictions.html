🎯 Scoring Methodology

⚡ Technical (35%)
Position-weighted attribute profile. Forwards weighted on shooting/dribbling/pace. Midfielders on passing/vision/stamina. Defenders on defending/strength/tackling.
🎯 Tier Fit (20%)
Match between player ability and team competition level. Prevents over/under-qualified recommendations. Aligned with FA pyramid tiers.
📅 Age Curve (15%)
U18s score highest (100pts). Peak at 18-21. Decreasing scores post-27. Aligned with UEFA talent development windows.
💪 Physical (10%)
Height and build optimality per position. Centre-backs favour height. Forwards favour athletic build. Based on FA player development data.
📊 Match Stats (7%)
Goals/appearances ratio for forwards, assists ratio for midfielders, clean sheet ratio for defenders and GKs. Real match performance signal.
🎯 Weakness Bonus (8%)
Players who can exploit your team weaknesses gain up to +8 points. Set piece threats, pace against high line, strength vs physical vulnerability.
