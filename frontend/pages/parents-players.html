Parents & players

Helping grassroots players get seen more safely

ScoutLink helps U7-U16 grassroots players be represented through coach-led profiles, structured evidence and verified scout access, rather than open self-promotion.

What families should know

  • Player information should be uploaded only with the right permissions or notices in place.
  • Compatibility scoring supports decisions; it does not decide a child's value or future.
  • Privacy and concern routes are available publicly.

How ScoutLink protects the experience

Coach-led profiles

Profiles are built by authorised adults around football evidence, not by children trying to promote themselves.

Verified scout access

ScoutLink access is controlled so player visibility is not a public open directory.

Clear report routes

Families can report safeguarding, misuse, privacy or platform concerns through public forms.

Frequently asked questions

Can ScoutLink guarantee a player will be scouted?

No. ScoutLink creates better evidence and visibility for verified scouting review, but it does not guarantee trials, signings or outcomes.

Who creates player profiles?

ScoutLink is coach-led. Authorised coaches, schools, academies or teams create and manage profiles.
