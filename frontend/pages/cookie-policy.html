Cookie Policy

Cookies and product analytics

Last updated: July 2026. ScoutLink uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the service secure, remember preferences, support logged-in sessions and understand product usage.

Essential cookies

These support login sessions, navigation, security, role-based access and core product operation.

Analytics cookies

Where enabled, product analytics help us understand how pages and workflows are used so we can improve ScoutLink. Sensitive form content, CV files and safeguarding descriptions should not be sent to analytics tools.

Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of ScoutLink from working properly.
