Accessibility at ScoutLink

ScoutLink aims to keep public information, registration, trust and contact routes usable across desktop and mobile devices, including keyboard access and readable form states.

Readable content

Public pages use clear headings, high-contrast text, plain language and visible focus states where possible.

Forms

Registration, contact, privacy and concern forms use labels, validation messages and appropriately sized tap targets.

Support

If you experience an accessibility barrier, contact us and include the page, device and assistive technology if relevant.

Contact us about accessibility

Use the contact form and choose accessibility or platform support as the reason. We review accessibility feedback as part of product quality and safety.
