Grassroots football intelligence

One place for player evidence, scouting and safer visibility.

ScoutLink helps coaches maintain youth football player profiles, fixtures, match facts and video evidence while reviewed scouts search, compare and shortlist players through controlled access.

  • Coach-led player evidence and football development records.
  • Reviewed scout access for grassroots football scouting workflows.
  • Compatibility scoring that supports decisions without replacing judgement.
  • Safeguarding-conscious routes for contact, concerns and visibility.

ScoutLink is operated by Stratex Analytics for coaches, reviewed scouts, players and internal product operations.
