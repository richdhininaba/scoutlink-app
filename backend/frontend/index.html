FAQ

Questions before you register

Is ScoutLink for U7 to U16 players?

Yes. Public ScoutLink messaging is focused on U7 to U16 grassroots football evidence and visibility.

Who can access player profiles?

Access is role-based and controlled. Verified scouts and authorised users only see information permitted by ScoutLink account rules.

Can parents or players register directly?

ScoutLink is coach-led. Families can review notices, raise privacy requests and report concerns through the public trust routes.

How does compatibility scoring work?

Scores compare available evidence against role and team-fit criteria. They support decisions; they do not guarantee selection or opportunities.
