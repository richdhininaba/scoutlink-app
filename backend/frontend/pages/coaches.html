What coaches can do in ScoutLink

Create structured profiles

Store player positions, physical context, coach-rated attributes, videos and profile completion in one place.

Add match evidence

Record match facts and player ratings so performance context is more useful than a single number.

Help scouts understand fit

Verified scouts can search and compare available evidence without direct open access to children.

Safe claim

ScoutLink does not guarantee trials, signings or scouting outcomes. It helps coaches create better evidence and gives verified scouts structured decision support.
