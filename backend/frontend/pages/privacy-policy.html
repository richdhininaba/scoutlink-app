How ScoutLink handles personal data

Last updated: July 2026. This page explains what ScoutLink collects, why it is used, who can access it and how people can exercise their rights.

Who controls the data

Stratex Analytics is the data controller for ScoutLink platform, registration, careers and safeguarding data.

What we collect

Account, organisation, player profile, Match Facts, ratings, fixtures, video, searches, pipeline activity, messages, exports, predictions, registration, careers and trust-request information.

Why we collect it

To operate ScoutLink, support coach-led evidence, review scout access, maintain security, prevent misuse, manage safeguarding, support recruitment and improve the product.

Registered-user platform activity

Logins, page views, searches, profile views, updates, uploads, notes, shortlists, interest, exports, predictions and admin decisions may be recorded for operation, security and audit.

Who can access data

Access depends on role and permission. Coaches manage their teams, reviewed scouts use approved workflows and Stratex administrators access records required for their duties.

Sharing and processors

Trusted providers include Supabase, Vercel, SendGrid and Heap. ScoutLink does not sell personal data.

Product analytics and cookies

Analytics helps understand product use and improve quality. Sensitive form content, CV files and safeguarding descriptions should not be sent to analytics tools.

Safeguarding and security logs

Restricted logs may include action type, user role, timestamp and affected record IDs for safety, compliance and misuse investigations.

Compatibility scoring

Compatibility is decision support. It does not decide whether a player will be selected, signed, trialled or offered an opportunity.

Retention

Records are kept only as long as required for the platform purpose, legal obligations, safeguarding, security, support or recruitment administration.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, objection or restriction. Parents and guardians can raise requests about youth-player data.
