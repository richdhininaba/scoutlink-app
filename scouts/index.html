Scout workflows built around evidence.

How compatibility works

Football compatibility scoring compares a player profile with scout setup inputs such as team weaknesses, role expectations, search preferences and long-term goals. The score is decision support, not an automatic selection or signing decision.

  • Team need fit
  • Role and formation fit
  • Match evidence and data confidence
  • Coach-rated attributes and development profile

Why access is reviewed

ScoutLink is designed for reviewed football scouts and controlled grassroots player visibility. Organisation, role and verification information supports responsible access before player search, comparison and communication tools are made available.

  • Coach-mediated scout interest
  • Visible safeguarding and concern routes
  • No open public directory of children
  • Structured evidence maintained by coaches
