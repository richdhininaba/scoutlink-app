How football evidence is collected and controlled

Last updated: June 2026. This page explains coach-led player data, consent expectations, youth visibility and platform records.

Important for coaches

Only add player information where you hold the appropriate authority and parent, guardian, club or school permission.

Who we are

ScoutLink is operated by Stratex Analytics. Stratex Analytics acts as the data controller for ScoutLink platform data.

What data we collect

Player names, age groups, positions, physical ranges, performance ratings, Match Facts, statistics, approved video and account or organisation information.

Parental and guardian consent

Coaches must hold the appropriate written or verifiable permission before submitting youth-player information and must be able to confirm that authority.

How player data is used

To support reviewed scouting access, player comparison, compatibility and development analysis, aggregate reporting, product operation and safeguarding.

Data retention

Records are retained only while needed for the platform purpose, account operation, legal duties, security or safeguarding, and may be removed following a valid request.

Safeguarding and security

Player visibility is controlled. Approved video remains permissioned. Platform data is protected using access control, encryption and restricted administration.

Your rights

Players, parents, guardians and account holders can request access, correction, restriction or deletion using the public privacy route.

Coach obligations

Coaches must add only authorised player information, keep it accurate, respect consent changes and notify ScoutLink when data should be corrected or removed.

Contact

Data questions can be sent to info@scoutlink.app.
