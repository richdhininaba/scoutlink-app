Cookies and product analytics

Last updated: July 2026. ScoutLink uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the service secure, remember preferences, support sessions and understand product usage.

Essential cookies

These support logged-in sessions, navigation, security, role-based access and core product operation.

Analytics cookies

Where enabled, analytics helps understand how pages and workflows are used. Sensitive form content, CV files and safeguarding descriptions should not be sent to analytics tools.

Managing cookies

Cookies can be managed through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of ScoutLink from working properly.
