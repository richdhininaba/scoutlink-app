Using ScoutLink responsibly

Last updated: July 2026. These terms apply to coaches, scouts, players, Stratex administrators and public users.

No guaranteed outcome

ScoutLink does not guarantee trials, contracts, academy selection, recruitment outcomes or professional opportunities.

Account eligibility

Users must provide accurate information and use the correct account type. Coach and scout access may be reviewed, approved, suspended or removed.

User role responsibilities

  • Coaches must be authorised to manage the player records they add.
  • Appropriate parent, guardian, club or school permission is required.
  • Scouts must use the platform for legitimate football scouting and recruitment.
  • Administrators must access only the data needed for their role.

Misuse of player data

Users must not scrape, mass-download, resell, share or export player information outside approved ScoutLink workflows or their authorised organisation.

No direct contact with minors

ScoutLink does not permit direct adult-to-child recruitment messaging. Interest should remain coach, club, school or guardian mediated.

False identity and abuse

False identities, abusive conduct, discrimination, unsafe use and unlawful activity may lead to suspension or account removal.

Platform activity and audit trails

ScoutLink may record account activity to operate and secure the service, support safeguarding, investigate misuse and maintain audit trails.

Intellectual property

ScoutLink software, scoring logic, product design, brand and materials belong to Stratex Analytics or its licensors.

Suspension and removal

Access may be restricted where permissions are disputed, safety concerns arise, data is misused, verification fails or these terms are breached.

Liability and governing law

ScoutLink is a decision-support platform and does not control third-party recruitment outcomes. The terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
