ScoutLink Data Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This policy applies to all coaches, scouts, and administrators using the ScoutLink platform.

Important Notice for Coaches

By registering on ScoutLink you confirm that you have obtained verifiable parental or guardian consent for all players aged 6 to 16. This is a legal requirement under UK GDPR, the Children Act 1989, and KCSIE (Keeping Children Safe in Education) guidelines.

Cookies and product analytics

ScoutLink uses cookies and product analytics to understand how people use the platform, improve features, protect user welfare, monitor suspicious activity and make the product safer and easier to use. If you register or sign in, we may link your platform activity to your account so we can support your experience and improve the service. You can read more in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

1. Who We Are

ScoutLink is operated by Stratex Analytics. We act as a data controller under UK GDPR. Contact us at info@scoutlink.app for any data-related queries.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following data about players: full name, date of birth, nationality, email address or parent/guardian email address (for players aged 6-16), position, physical attributes (described using ranges, not precise measurements), performance ratings, match statistics, video content, and avatar configuration. We do not collect photographs of players.

3. Parental Consent - Players Aged 6 to 16

For any player under the age of 16, coaches must obtain explicit written or verifiable parental/guardian consent before submitting their information. By checking the consent box during registration, the coach confirms this consent has been obtained and is on file. ScoutLink reserves the right to remove any player data where consent cannot be confirmed. Parent/guardian email addresses will only be used for notifications directly relevant to their child's profile.

4. How We Use Player Data

Player data is used exclusively for: (a) enabling scouts at professional and semi-professional clubs to identify talent; (b) generating AI-driven compatibility, development and transfer value assessments; (c) producing aggregate statistics and performance analytics. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Player profiles are retained for as long as the associated coach account is active, or until deletion is requested. Upon request, all data can be removed within 30 days in accordance with the Right to Erasure under UK GDPR.

6. Safeguarding

ScoutLink does not permit players to upload photographs of themselves. Avatar images are computer-generated only. Video content is voluntary and subject to coach/guardian approval. All data is stored on EU servers (Supabase, AWS eu-central-1) and protected by industry-standard encryption.

7. Your Rights

Players, parents, and coaches may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their data at any time by emailing info@scoutlink.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Coaches' Obligations

By registering, coaches agree to: (a) only add players for whom they hold appropriate consent; (b) keep player information accurate and up to date; (c) not add any player's photograph to the platform; (d) notify ScoutLink immediately if any player or parent/guardian withdraws consent. Breach of these obligations may result in immediate suspension of the coach account.

9. Contact

Terms of Use

By using ScoutLink you agree to use the platform responsibly, keep account access secure, enter accurate information, and avoid uploading unlawful, unsafe or misleading content. ScoutLink may restrict access where safeguarding, data protection or platform integrity concerns arise.
