What the demo shows

ScoutLink is a football scouting platform and player-development platform for grassroots talent identification. It gives coaches a structured way to maintain player evidence and gives reviewed scouts a clearer alternative or companion workflow alongside tools such as Wyscout, Tonsser and football player databases built for older or professional-market use cases.

Coach-led evidence

Player profiles, match facts, fixtures and video evidence are maintained by teams rather than scraped from open public directories.

Compatibility support

Football compatibility scoring helps scouts compare player fit, team needs and evidence confidence while leaving final judgement with people.

Controlled contact

Scout interest, messaging and recruitment pipelines are handled through safer, coach-mediated routes for youth football visibility.
