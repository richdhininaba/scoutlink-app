Trust and safeguarding

Player visibility should never feel uncontrolled.

Scout access is reviewed, player visibility is controlled, scout interest is coach-mediated, and ScoutLink does not support open scout-to-child contact. Families and teams can understand data use and raise concerns through public routes.

Common questions

Before you start.

Who is ScoutLink for?

ScoutLink supports grassroots coaches, reviewed scouts, clubs, schools, academies, parents and players working around U7 to U16 evidence.

Can anyone browse players?

No. ScoutLink is designed around reviewed access, controlled visibility and safer adult-led contact routes.

Does compatibility scoring replace scout judgement?

No. Compatibility scoring supports decisions by making evidence easier to compare and explain.
